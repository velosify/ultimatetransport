How a ride works

Three steps, start to finish.

  1. STEP 1

    Call and book

    Give us the date, the pickup address, where you’re headed, and what time you need to be there. We’ll quote the fare on that same call. A day or two of notice helps, but ask about same‑day — sometimes we can make it work.

  2. STEP 2

    We come to your door

    The driver meets you at the door, not just the curb, and helps you down the ramp and into the van. Your chair is locked down with a four‑point securement system and you wear a lap and shoulder belt, same as any seat.

  3. STEP 3

    In, and back again

    We walk you in to the front desk if you’d like. When you’re done, call and we’ll come back for the return trip. A family member or aide is welcome to ride along.
